Downloaded 2026-10-13. Original 2K JPEG files retained without modification.
Runtime presentation adapts these equirectangular maps by sampling them onto
spherical planets, changing viewpoint and adding simplified solar illumination.
The source maps use NASA imagery/elevation data, adjusted colors and fictional
terrain to fill unmapped gaps. These renders are models, not new mission photos.
Venus uses the atmosphere map. Earth uses the day map (no live cloud/weather data).

Obliquities: NASA Planetary Fact Sheet (degrees)
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/
Mercury 0.034; Venus 177.4; Earth 23.4; Mars 25.2;
Jupiter 3.1; Saturn 26.7; Uranus 97.8; Neptune 28.3.
All orbital inclinations are zero in this game. Pole azimuth and texture rotation
are illustrative fixed values, not actual date-dependent orientation data.
